### Design notes on selection behavior (not problems, just so review isn't surprised)
- **Arc always outranks contextual** for the same speaker (source rank). So `sophia-arithmetic-zero` (contextual) only surfaces when Sophia has no eligible arc beat. Intended by the system; noted so it isn't read as a bug.
- **Same-character arc ordering** relies on `priority` + `once`, not flag-chaining. Where two arc beats share a gate (Mara `sings`/`crisis` both on `b1`; Sophia `method`/`outpaced` both on `b3`), I gave the earlier beat higher priority so it fires first; after it's seen, the next becomes top-eligible. I deliberately **did not invent an arc-flag namespace** to hard-chain them (the spine uses letter-ids like `b1`; there's no convention for arc-internal flags). If you want guaranteed order regardless of priority, add `sets_flag` to the earlier and gate the later on it.
- No `cooldown_runs` set on arc beats — the engine defaults arc to 1 (never two of a character's arc beats back-to-back). Barks use `cooldown_runs: 2` (matching the shipped `wren-bark-maps`), except three lower-frequency Wren/idle barks at `3`.
