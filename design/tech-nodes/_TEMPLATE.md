# Tech Node — <NAME>

> Canonical format for a tech-tree node. Copy this file to author a new one. Every node is **explanation → puzzle → "aha" → unlock**, framed in-fiction. Keep the science *true*; keep the voice warm, witty, Tycho-curious — not a textbook. The puzzle must require genuine *understanding*, not trivia.

## 2. One-line pitch
What the player *learns* and what they *get*, in a sentence.

## 3. Framing / trigger
When the node becomes available, who introduces it, and the motivation (why the town wants it now). 1–3 short dialogue snippets max.

## 4. Explanation (read before the puzzle)
The readable real history + hard science the player sees when the node is ready to research. Tight, delightful, accurate. This is the "tension/cerebral" beat — make it earn its place.

## 5. The puzzle
- **Type:** (interactive sandbox / arrange / predict / quiz-with-reasoning …)
- **Setup:** the situation presented.
- **Player actions:** what they do.
- **Win condition:** what counts as solved.
- **Principle tested:** the true idea the puzzle forces them to grasp.
- **Failure feedback:** what wrong attempts show (teach through failure).
- **Difficulty / graceful path:** Linnea's hints (escalating) + auto-solve after ~N runs, with her actual lines. Reward thinking; never hard-gate.

## 6. The "aha" (post-solve)
The payoff reveal delivered *after* solving — the deeper unity or "oh, THAT's why." The "release" of the cerebral beat. This is the soul; spend effort here.

## 7. Unlock & in-fiction beat
What it grants mechanically, and the short story beat of receiving it.

## 8. Educational integrity
What's literally true, where we simplify (and that the simplification is honest), common misconceptions corrected, and sources. Players learn *true* things.

## 9. Production notes
Assets, UI, and interactions the dev needs. Flag where a "puzzle" can be a scripted state machine rather than a real simulation (vibecode-friendly).

## 10. Open questions / tuning
Anything unresolved or needing playtest tuning.
